# CM-03 native Sandbox account projection — source only

The Stage-G worker previously projected only Nautilus USDC balance_total. Native Sandbox position records expose gross USD realized PnL, individual USDC commissions, and USD unrealized PnL at a mark. The new worker read model uses those records and the same native account for settled cash, free, locked, realized net of fees, fees, marked unrealized and equity. It adds unrealized to settled cash exactly once. The account's locked balance is not labelled initial or maintenance margin; those remain unknown. An open position with a missing, future or >120-second venue mark has null unrealized/equity and STALE_OR_MISSING. A flat account has known zero unrealized from its native positions.

The source does not rewrite the installed worker or its historical fills. The installed open BTC/TP worker still has historic zero-fee economics and cannot be restarted safely without CM-05 native open-state recovery. No retroactive correction is claimed. Funding scheduled by public updates is not a payment; the existing worker funding_state remains explicit, and posted funding is not added a second time by this projection. Realized position PnL is net of native commissions and does not include a separate funding claim.

The new focused projection test covers entry, partial TP, adverse mark, stale mark and flat close with USDC values. Generated runtime OpenAPI and TypeScript contract updated. ## Correction after native close and reopen

A disposable pinned 1.231 NETTING node exposed two faults in the first projection: a completed cycle moves from cache.positions() to cache.position_snapshots() on reopen, and native Position.realized_pnl is gross USD for the public HL instrument while commissions are USDC. The corrected read model includes closed native snapshots plus current positions, deduplicated by native trade IDs. It converts native USD PnL using only the required 1:1 model USD/USDC quote and subtracts native USDC commissions once. Other currencies and missing model FX fail closed. Native account cash remains the authoritative settled total; funding is not inferred from a cash difference.

The disposable native regression verifies flat then reopened cycles. Flat native cash 9869.732485 equals 10000 + 50.10 USD gross at model 1:1 minus 180.367515 USDC fees. After another 0.01 BTC entry, the prior closed cycle is in position_snapshots(), current position holds the new fee, and native cash 9869.462431 equals 10000 + 50.10 - 180.637569. The projection reports the same cumulative realized net and fee values; native free and locked balances match. The standing CM-05 replay probe still produces an immediate full TP fill at one quote timestamp; no open-partial restart claim follows.
