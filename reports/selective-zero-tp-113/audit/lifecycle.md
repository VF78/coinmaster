## Confirmed findings

F1 — **Observed q1 parent-notional cap breach.** At strategy 651-681 q0 saves gross lots from requested notional; 699-723 preserves them; 875-894 submits exact lots at q1; 927-942 checks minimum, entry gates and margin but not candidate.max_parent_notional. Three-quote Nautilus repro: cap/request 100, q0 price100, q1 price101, 1 BTC filled for 101 with no gate block. In the actual selective report, 11/105 BTC entries exceed the 10,000,000 cap, all full-fallback; total excess 39,923.3110, largest 13,629.2984 (0.1363%). Smallest systematic correction: recheck max parent notional using q1 executable price before native submission; with fixed saved lots, fail closed on excess (or make a separately approved sizing policy). Native margin success does not establish the parent cap.

F2 — **Pre-fill touch can activate a later SOL right.** Strategy 729-733 records reference touch even if no BTC entry fill / earned fraction; 743-755 later activates from that stored bit after a real partial fill on a non-touch quote. Callback-order replay: before fill, touch sets touched=true, rights empty; then real 0.288/0.576 entry earns fraction0.5; next quote below reference activates level0 without a post-fill touch. This ordering was not observed in the saved run: all 105 entries filled at q1, and 76/76 omitted rights activated exactly once. Smallest correction: record the touch only after a confirmed positive net entry/fraction exists, or bind touch timestamp to confirmed fill time and require causal order. No claim of native-exchange reproduction for this edge.

F3 — **Ordinary SOL z-exit IOC partial leaves residual without retry.** Domain 431-449 requests full z exit; strategy 1350-1377 and 1457-1484 rearm only SOL_HARD_TIMEOUT on terminal/cancel, not SOL_Z_FULL_EXIT. Five-quote Nautilus native repro: open 2,000 SOL; z-full IOC requests 2,000, native fills 1,000 and cancels; after two further SOL quotes, 1,000 remains, domain pending empty, mandatory exit none, queue empty. A later daily z condition may retry, but a rebound can leave the requested exit incomplete. Saved selective run's one z-full and one z-half fill were each 500, so no observed PnL impact. Smallest correction: retain an exit-decision residual owner after partial/cancel with bounded quote retry and forced/timeout priority; distinguish conditional z signal from mandatory timeout.

F4 — **Terminal-flat reports success while an unsubmitted entry is queued.** Strategy 1153-1165 returns FLAT on zero native positions without discarding queued q0 BTC_ENTRY/domain pending/selection plan. State callback repro: terminal status FLAT with queued=1, domain pending entry=1, deferred plan=1. q1 submission guard at 690 prevents an entry at/after terminal, but no further quote leaves orphaned intent state; a native entry already submitted with unknown ACK is a separate unresolved race. Smallest correction: terminalize queued pre-submit intents before declaring FLAT, and separately reconcile inflight native orders before terminal completeness.

## Requested branch classification

- Ordinary SOL half IOC: `on_fill` accumulates `sol_half_filled_lots` and marks half done only at target; partial-cancel clears the parent, keeps the native/domain residual, and a *later accepted daily decision* with continuing half-z can issue remaining target lots. It has no quote-level residual rearm. Ordinary z-full also has no quote-level rearm (F3); another daily full-z decision can issue current remaining native quantity only if half is already complete and z remains beyond full threshold. Mandatory hard timeout is different: cancel/reject/partial terminal calls `_rearm_mandatory_sol_exit`, which reads actual native SOL leaves and retries after 60 seconds on SOL quotes. Thus F3 is an incomplete conditional exit under a partial IOC, not an unconditional claim that risk is never later closed.
- Group/regime/trail/forced close: `CLOSE_ALL` discards all queued intents and their deferred selections, terminalizes each domain parent, clears timeout ownership, cancels native resting orders, then sizes reduce-only closes from actual native cache after cancellation. Regime reentry occurs only after `_reconcile_group_flat` confirms both native positions/orders flat. Native submitted unknown ACK remains in domain pending, so no new ordinary entry is manufactured before authoritative terminal; late ACK versus terminal boundary was not reproduced.
- Entry cancel/reject/deny: native canceled or rejected/expired parent removes pending mapping and calls domain terminal; zero-confirmed-fill empty episode is released, while partial confirmed entry retains episode and replans only real BTC TP leaves. Local min-lot/min-notional/admission or durable-prep denial calls `_deny_local_entry` and terminates the intent without a synthetic fill. Deferred q0 selection similarly keeps one pending parent until q1 or a terminal/forced/deposit discard; F4 is the terminal-flat early-return exception.
