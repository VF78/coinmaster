2026-10-22. Scope: frozen E1 protocol, report/catalogue QA and source at HEAD `0a9a635`; no price reads, targets, training, predictions or evaluator reruns. Findings distinguish demonstrated behavior from plausible explanations. Full 699-day period and pilot remain unchanged.

The defensible outcome is **no demonstrated conditional forecast improvement**. Phase is worse than prior on both aggregate co-primary errors; every primary paired interval includes zero. This does not establish that event shapes contain no information or that one implementation choice caused failure.

## One proposed controlled hypothesis — preliminary support only

The fixed 25/75 phase/prior blend is **not recommended for a new run**. Its tiny saved-row gain has paired intervals crossing zero and an in-sample Brier improvement ceiling along that direction of only about 0.0676%. This supports avoiding additional budget for global shrinkage alone.

The candidate now under review is a four-cell conditional empirical prior. Partition only by completed-leg direction ±1 and the existing frozen threshold/current sigma24 ratio at the fixed boundary 1. These are observed inputs; no new shape, event timing, label or market representation is introduced. The cell label is observable at confirmation and uses no future information. Conditioning on direction is not equivalent to mirroring long/short labels; retain absolute side definitions.

For each query, build its empirical conditional distribution solely from **eligible historical events in that query’s cell**, then shrink that distribution toward the empirical prior on the identical entire eligible past pool. Use one fixed nonnegative event-weight vector for every side/SL outcome. Derive all 18 quantiles by inverse weighted CDF and all 6 touch probabilities by weighted averages. This preserves quantile, wider-SL MFE and wider-SL touch ordering inherited from individual targets. The scalar cell itself is an economic hypothesis about threshold/current volatility state, not an automatically meaningful regime identifier.

Saved `stats-four-cell.csv` diagnostics provide bounded preliminary support: pooled cell counts down/low 57, down/high 41, up/low 55, up/high 45; cell-by-fold counts 10–23. In down/low events the global prior underpredicts long adverse touch at all 3 SL levels in all 3 folds; up/low short Q50 and up/high long Q80 show consistently low empirical quantile coverage. These recurring conditional residuals motivate a low-complexity conditional distribution more directly than simply shrinking shape forecasts. However, only 8/24 SL coordinates and 17/72 quantile-coverage coordinates retain a strictly common residual sign across all 3 folds. Some pooled effects reverse by fold, so this is a weak exploratory rationale, not established stable regime information. All four cells must be retained rather than choosing the apparently favorable residuals. Fix the shrinkage pseudo-count once in the preregistration; no coefficient grid, cell threshold fitting, recursive splits, cell selection or dropping sparse cells. Evaluation cell counts do not establish the size or independence of each historical training cell; those must be reported from past eligible IDs in the eventual authorized run. Empty-cell fallback must be the same eligible global prior and remain in the all-query denominator. Treat the full four-cell definition as one hypothesis, not separate subgroups to promote selectively.

Strict-purge bookkeeping must first be corrected for every control and treatment, including conservative direct support min(activation−169h, anchor−1h, existing context support). Document the recursive event-selection history convention separately. This corrects historical label/feature disjointness; it is not evidence of future-price leakage. Compare the conditional prior with the same-causality global prior and original phase model; keep the immutable E1 record historical.

Proposed exploratory gate: both co-primary proper losses improve against the same-causality global prior, with 14d paired upper 95% bounds < 0 and at least2/3 folds negative for both; report the comparison with phase and 28d sensitivity without hiding regressions. Retain all 198 eligible mature IDs and full 699-day period. Report four-cell counts, effective label weights, fallback, all 18 quantile calibration/ties, all 6 touch calibration and coherence. Reused DEVELOPMENT diagnostics select the hypothesis post hoc, so any further result on those folds remains exploratory; CHECK and independent-validation claims remain out of scope. Source changes, forecast construction and reruns require root/owner approval.

## Narrow primary-source check

1. [Meinshausen, Quantile Regression Forests (JMLR 2006), especially §§1,3](https://jmlr.org/papers/v7/meinshausen06a.html): conditional quantiles can be obtained by inversion of a weighted empirical conditional distribution; pinball loss evaluates the stated quantile target. Its forest assumptions and consistency do not transfer automatically to 32 time-dependent BTC neighbors. No recommendation to train a forest is made here.
2. [Gneiting and Ranjan, Combining Predictive Distributions (2011 preprint)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1106.1638), §§2–3: pooling distributions has calibration/dispersion limitations. A valid mixture CDF is not necessarily a calibrated or information-theoretically coherent forecast. This cautions against treating stronger shrinkage as guaranteed improvement; it is separate from simple cross-SL monotonicity above.
3. [Bagnall and Lines, An Experimental Evaluation of Nearest Neighbour Time Series Classification (2014)](https://arxiv.org/abs/1406.4757): distance/warping choice and training-only validation matter empirically in time-series classification. The experiments concern classification benchmarks, not financial future-path forecasting, and provide no evidence that phase alignment or DTW will improve this experiment.

No additional literature search, model family change or economic test is proposed.
