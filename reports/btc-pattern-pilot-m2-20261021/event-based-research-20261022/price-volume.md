# Price and volume in causal wave/correction learning

Research note, 2026-10-22. Proposal only: no new market-data scan, API request, fit, forecast, backtest, CHECK access, VPS action, pilot action, or source edit. Static source inspection and primary-literature browsing only. Scope remains BTC DEVELOPMENT `[2024-06-01,2026-05-01)` UTC. This note adds volume to the proposed price-event design; it does not establish predictive skill or authorize execution.

## Six primary sources and their limits

1. **Blume, Easley & O'Hara (1994), Market Statistics and Technical Analysis: The Role of Volume.** [Publisher paper/abstract](https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/pdf/10.1111/j.1540-6261.1994.tb04424.x). Their equilibrium model allows price-volume sequences to reveal information about signal precision beyond price alone. This supplies a reason to test joint inputs. It is a theoretical mechanism under model assumptions, not evidence for a BTC correction rule or a universal positive volume-return relationship.

2. **Cont, Kukanov & Stoikov (2014), The Price Impact of Order Book Events.** [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/1011.6402), [journal DOI](https://doi.org/10.1093/jjfinec/nbt003). For 50 US stocks, short-interval price changes relate more robustly to best-quote order-flow imbalance than to trade volume. Depth matters. This concerns contemporaneous microstructure relations, not an established future BTC event forecast. OHLCV cannot reconstruct additions/cancellations, book depth, OFI, or aggressor direction; `sign(close-open)*V` is a candle-direction proxy, not true signed trade flow or OFI.

3. **Easley, López de Prado & O'Hara (2012), The Volume Clock: Insights into the High Frequency Paradigm.** [Author SSRN record](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2034858), [institutional publication record](https://opus.lib.uts.edu.au/handle/10453/114992). Motivates activity-based clocks and microstructure measured by traded quantity. It does not show that thresholded volume bars guarantee stationary observations or better correction forecasts. The SSRN search record/abstract was accessible; direct fetch returned 403, and the institutional full text is closed. This note therefore does not claim a full-text audit or rely on detailed numerical results from it.

4. **Jiang, Kelly & Xiu (2023), (Re-)Imag(in)ing Price Trends.** [Author/institution-hosted published paper](https://economics.yale.edu/sites/default/files/2023-11/The%20Journal%20of%20Finance%20-%202023%20-%20JIANG%20-%20Re%25E2%2580%2590%20Imag%20in%20ing%20Price%20Trends_0.pdf), [DOI](https://doi.org/10.1111/jofi.13268). CNN inputs include daily OHLC, volume bars and a moving-average line; the paper describes sensitivity checks excluding volume. Supports treating volume as an explicit representation choice and controlling its contribution. US-stock panel return prediction is a different population/target from one BTC perpetual, intraday causal waves, or 3TP/SL. No transfer or volume-only effect is established here.

5. **Cong, Li, Tang & Yang, Crypto Wash Trading.** [Author preprint](https://arxiv.org/abs/2108.10984), [author publication list](https://www.linwilliamcong.com/research). Their exchange study documents fabricated reported activity and price distortion. It motivates venue-specific provenance and skepticism toward pooled reported volume. It is not a measurement of manipulation in this pinned Bybit dataset; its cross-exchange estimates must not be assigned to these BTC bars.

6. **Bybit official API documentation, Get Kline.** [Official schema](https://bybit-exchange.github.io/docs/v5/market/kline). Distinguishes base volume from quote turnover for linear contracts, reverses their units for inverse contracts, and warns that an unclosed candle's close is its current last-trade price. Operational consequence: pin venue/category/symbol/units, aggregate only completed observations, and do not pool spot/perpetual/inverse volume by adding incomparable numbers.

Taken together, these sources motivate a falsifiable volume ablation. Volume can reflect activity, liquidity demand, information arrival, liquidation or venue artifacts; none of the sources licenses “large volume means continuation” or “low-volume correction means certain reversal.”

## Six bounded feature candidates

These are proposed measurements, not claims of structural causality. “Causal” means computable from information available by decision time. Start from price-defined DC events, with threshold frozen or scaled by **prior** volatility, confirmation time distinct from retrospective extreme time, and the current phase represented only up to the observed prefix. Use one pinned closed-bar resolution. A bar's volume belongs to the information set only at its close; do not allocate unknown intrabar volume around a high/low pivot.

Let `I` be the available impulse phase and `C(t)` the available correction prefix; `D_S` is each phase's elapsed duration, `V_S=sum(V_i)` its observed base volume, and `B_S=sum(b_i)` its expected volume. Define `b_i` from a frozen past-only seasonal baseline: median volume for that UTC hour-of-week over the prior eight complete weeks ending **before event start**, with a preregistered minimum support and no adaptive full-period fitting. This is one proposed baseline, not a parameter grid. Sum expected quantities over the actual included intervals; do not compare unequal-duration raw totals.

| Candidate | Definition and purpose |
|---|---|
| 1. Impulse activity intensity | `log((V_I+eps)/(B_I+eps))`; compares volume rate against duration- and calendar-matched expected activity. Report `D_I` separately in both arms. |
| 2. Correction-prefix intensity | Same expression over `C(t)` only. An unfinished pullback contributes its observed volume, never its eventual total. |
| 3. Relative phase activity | `log((V_C/D_C+eps)/(V_I/D_I+eps))`; measures whether the correction trades faster/slower than the impulse, controlling separately for duration. The difference of candidates 2 and 1 is a seasonally adjusted companion, not a separately tuned feature family. |
| 4. Impulse price/volume efficiency | `abs(log(P_I,end/P_I,start))/sigma_pre / max(V_I/B_I,eps)`; distinguishes large displacement on modest activity from heavy activity with little displacement. This is a descriptive ratio, not an estimated causal impact coefficient. |
| 5. Correction relative efficiency | The analogous prefix ratio divided by impulse efficiency, with a preregistered floor and fit-only clipping. Treat tiny displacement/volume carefully; no fabricated infinite “illiquidity.” |
| 6. Prefix activity change | Log rate ratio of the last and first halves of the **observed** phase prefix, normalized by their expected seasonal volumes. Require two complete bars; otherwise mark unavailable. Do not split by half of eventual event duration. |

`eps`, minimum support and clipping rules are frozen before evaluation and scale-consistent; if chosen from data, use fit history only. A first bounded arm should use candidates 1–4 as one fixed bundle; reserve 5–6 as documented alternatives, not additional live trials or post-result substitutions. A phase is allowed to include directional-change confirmation bars under one explicit convention; neither arm may backdate its forecast to the extreme. Once an impulse extreme is confirmed, its past closed-bar attribution is known at confirmation time, not earlier.

Missing/corrupt bars are not zero-volume bars. Reject/invalidate the affected event prefix and required baseline according to one common coverage rule, preserve true zero-volume bars, and restart/warm up segmentation after gaps. Record bar age and maximum allowed staleness; never extend stale price/volume silently across outages. Missing aggressor labels, OFI, open interest and depth remain unavailable. A base-volume feature is local to the pinned venue/product; use true quote turnover only if explicitly present and separately proven, never silently substitute `V*OHLCmean`.

## One bounded event-ID ablation, not a volume-clock experiment

Proposed H2 uses exactly the same **price DC event IDs**, confirmation-time origins, eligible mature-prefix library, outcomes, chronological splits, purge/embargo, stage/direction/amplitude/duration/volatility matched controls, model family, fit/neighbor budget and random seed across three arms:

- **V0 / PRICE:** remove every volume input, including context `relative_volume20`, sequence channel six, rendered volume strips and any amount proxy/volume-derived embedding. Retaining a hidden volume-bearing representation would invalidate the name. Retain price/range/volatility/calendar/age controls.
- **Vbase / PRICE+existing VOL:** V0 plus the existing bar-volume anomalies and context anomaly, with one frozen mapping to the event prefix. Keep their historical warmup in the common support/purge rule. This measures the value of existing volume information in the event design.
- **Vevent / PRICE+existing VOL+phase VOL:** Vbase plus one fixed candidates-1–4 bundle above. This measures the incremental phase summary, including its extra normalization history. Use common eligibility/dependency spans for all arms so missing seasonal baseline support cannot alter evaluated IDs after outcomes are seen.

Freeze dimensions/scaling and equal procedure/budget before fitting; a compact regularized predictor or the already chosen event-prefix method suffices. No additional neural-family sweep, alternate volume source, threshold bank or normalization grid is implied. With retrieval, keep eligible library and matching controls identical; differences in neighbors produced by added volume coordinates are part of the representation effect and must be logged. Calendar normalization and minimum-history rules must be common and outcome-blind.

Primary contrasts are paired Vbase-minus-V0 and Vevent-minus-Vbase proper-score changes on identical IDs, with episode/calendar-block uncertainty and dependence-aware purging covering the full feature and outcome spans. Score the exact same frozen event-outcome target; if later targeting a joint 3TP/SL path, use the same path/order, censoring and entry contract in every arm. Report direction/phase calibration and coverage separately; no conversion of a directional or quantile improvement into ROI. Predeclare one primary score and the two ordered contrasts, record all attempts, and require stability across held-out time blocks rather than a favorable aggregate alone. No observed effect, sample count, optimal threshold, or test outcome is asserted by this proposal.

**Volume as a clock is a different intervention.** Stopping a bar/event after a quantity threshold changes timestamps, elapsed durations, target populations and possibly label overlap. Such an arm cannot be described as the same-ID feature ablation. With minute OHLCV, a threshold crossing is known only at minute close; exact within-minute volume-clock bars cannot be reconstructed. Any later separate clock experiment must compare both feature sets on the new common timestamp population and distinguish selection/timing from representation. It is excluded from the bounded H2 above.
