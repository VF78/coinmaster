- The full numeric representation has780coordinates versus204for the grouped version. Preserve the existing normalization/distance convention without silently introducing another block weighting. Results apply to that representation change, not a mathematically pure order-only intervention.
- ContextOnly uses batch64 to match original CNN optimizer-update exposure and auxiliary metric batch support. - Full-versus-grouped numeric loss is a mandatory paired comparison; a prior win alone does not establish an improvement from removing the averaging.
