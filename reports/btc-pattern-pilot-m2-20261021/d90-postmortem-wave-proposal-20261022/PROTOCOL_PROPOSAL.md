## Exact D1 detector

1. Use completed UTC D1 closes only. A bar's close becomes observable at its next UTC midnight. Let `x=log(close)` and freeze `δ=log(1.10)` for the entire period. Reversals are symmetric in log space; a downward threshold is a fall to `1/1.10` of the high, not an arithmetic10% fall. No volatility rescaling or duration constraint.
2. Initialise at the genuine May15 close in UNDECIDED mode, retaining running low and high. Replace an extreme only on a strict new extreme; equal prices keep the earliest timestamp. At each later close, a rise `x-low≥δ` confirms that low as the first pivot and enters UP mode, or a fall `high-x≥δ` confirms that high and enters DOWN mode. Both cannot first trigger on one close from a previously untriggered close-only prefix. Record the confirmation close/time separately from the pivot.
3. UP mode: retain the running highest close since the previous confirmation, updating it before the reversal check; the first close at leastδ below that extreme confirms a high B and changes to DOWN. Seed the new running low with that confirmation close. DOWN mode is exactly symmetric, confirming a low on a rise ofδ and seeding the new running high at the confirming close. Do not search for another pivot within the same bar.
4. Emit every consecutive confirmed-pivot triple A,B,C. Store each pivot's bar timestamp, price and confirmation timestamp. D is the confirmation timestamp/close of C, distinct from C. A→B is the main move; B→C is the correction, for both upward and downward orientations. Triples share legs; do not call them independent. Retain corrections that cross A, tiny/large/delayed corrections and all durations.
5. First pivot and any wave whose A precedes June1 remain LEFT_TRUNCATED/WARMUP_CONTEXT, not a full in-period training object. Retain them in the catalogue. The seed-to-first-pivot path is INITIALISATION, never a fictitious completed leg. Retain UNDECIDED prefixes, no-confirmed-B prefixes, a running B not yet confirmed, and a confirmed B whose C/D is still unknown. At May1 stop, keep right-censored and provisional states; never manufacture C at the last price. A full in-period wave has A≥June1 and D≤May1.
6. At every recorded historical prefix, store exact data availability and the detector's raw support from the May15 seed bar. The last unfinished state is replayed causally; final C/D and final phase fields must not populate earlier feature rows.

Stage A preserves all699 daily dates and maps the existing372 H1 catalogue IDs/198 scored IDs to the then-observable prefix for traceability, without changing any old ID or computing new targets. These old events are descriptive child references, not the new scoring population.
