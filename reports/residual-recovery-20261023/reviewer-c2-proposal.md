## Minimum contract if the owner ever revisits it separately

A global arm must be explicit account state persisting across actual group closes, consumed by a stated economic claim/submission rule and rearmed only by fresh settled DD<=35%; global H and the objective never reset. It must not reset on a new parent or silently become a fixed timer. Ordinary/mandatory exits keep their original priority.

One protective parent would freeze absolute native leaves once. Children must serialize on authoritative terminal/cancel/ACK, sum actual fills against the fixed target, and have a finite prespecified child/time bound; no repeated fraction of changing quantity, synthetic full-fill credit or repeated retry after an unknown ACK. An independent ordinary exit cancels/burns the unused parent/token. Restoration credit stays actual-fill-only, original BTC TP/SOL authority remains conserved, and the first eligible whole4h interval begins after the LAST actual protective fill AND the aggregate protective-parent terminal. Partial restoration remains bounded. These are feasible lifecycle requirements, not current implementation authorization or a reason to spend a slot now.
