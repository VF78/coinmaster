## Minimum lawful native state and transitions

One existing execution owner must retain the following episode/cycle-scoped facts: original episode and native opening identity; actual held lots and current entry completion; original BTC level allocation/TP completion and SOL earned/consumed/retired entitlement; outstanding orders and unresolved submits/cancels; frozen cut target; actual protective fill quantity/VWAP; residual restoration credit; cut/restore-attempt flags; independent exit invalidation; last eligible receipt and completed recovery observation. This is intent state for the existing executor, not a second portfolio or synthetic PnL book.

1. **Claim:** only fresh causal settled native account/marks, a completed accepted entry, positive equity, no conflicting pending opening/ordinary/mandatory work, and a matching native cycle can claim. Freeze an absolute lot target and set the once-cycle claim before invoking callbacks. Entry or account/mark gaps invalidate predecessor/crossing evidence; they must not manufacture a crossing. If a level-trigger rather than crossing is chosen, freeze that choice explicitly.
2. **Cancel / reduction:** cancel conflicting active leaves and await authoritative resolution. Reconcile any late normal fills against the fixed actual held-lot target. Unknown ACK is owned work, even if not visible in the open-order list. Submit one reduce-only IOC attempt from actual held leaves. A partial IOC creates credit only for actual protective fills; canceled/unfilled leaves create none and cannot trigger a percentage retry. Protective fills earn no TP completion or SOL right.
3. **Reduced / credit:** credit is tagged to the actual original lot/level authority. It is neither refunded SOL consumed notional nor a new earning event. Keep the original PLANREF, episode/gross plan, beta sizing anchor, SOL cycle age/frozen sigma, TP accounting and timeout. A SOL restore may reconstitute only previously authorized and actually removed lots; it cannot duplicate their original right or simultaneously consume the same allowance as a fresh add. Ordinary original adds remain bounded by their original allowance. Do not restore omitted/unfilled BTC allocations.
4. **Recovery admission:** one completed post-cut observation crosses the frozen recovery band in the same surviving valid episode; a strictly later fresh executable quote must still satisfy the condition. Simulate the proposed increase only for native admission/risk capacity, never as substitute PnL. Quantity is the minimum of actual unused cut-fill credit, surviving original authority and current native lot/margin/risk capacity. Reserve submitted restoration quantity before synchronous callbacks; until terminal resolution no duplicate restore or conflicting original add may spend that authority.
5. **Restoration:** one native IOC attempt, actual fills only. It is an increase, never reduce-only. Partial canceled leaves receive no retry, and the attempt remains spent. Charge actual native price/commission; native position VWAP changes while original management anchors remain fixed. Restore active TP leaves only against confirmed current quantity, assigning restored lots to their surviving original levels without enlarging cumulative original TP authority. No duplicate TP fill credit or SOL right can arise from round-tripping protective/restored lots.
6. **Preemption / expiry:** an independent ordinary exit decision, actual ordinary partial fill, mandatory regime/trail/timeout/liquidation exit, flat reconciliation or changed cycle irrevocably revokes restoration credit. Technical recreation of the same pre-cut passive TP is not a new independent exit, but marketable, queued or unknown ordinary exits block restoration. Mandatory exits preempt immediately; unresolved protective/restore orders remain owned until reconciled, and late fills are included in actual close leaves. Once invalidated, credit must never resurrect through a late callback. No timer, fresh-parent credit transfer, peak reset, second cut or second restore is allowed.

The narrow contract is one cut attempt plus one restoration attempt per native cycle, not one successful full fill of each. Partial fills, rejects and local denials must have explicit attempt-spending semantics. A new group can receive its ordinary E4 lifecycle; no old restoration credit survives it. Any account loss-budget state used for admission must not silently reset at group close.

## Failure modes and required focused proof

Native fixtures should prove late TP fill during cancel, partial protective/restore IOC, reject/deny, unknown submit/cancel ACK, duplicate callback, stale receipt/fill epoch, invalid execution-time reclaim, fresh discretionary add competing for SOL allowance, changed episode and lot rounding/dust. Test mandatory exit before admission, during cancel wait and after a restoration submit, including a late increase fill. State transitions must conserve original level authority and actual fill credit and keep the native account authoritative. These are lifecycle proofs, not a new historical run.

Economic risks remain: reclaim whipsaw; fees and lost movement while reduced; daily confirmation arriving too late; restoring risk before a second fall; risk transfers from SOL to BTC through changed capital; realized losses across multiple parents; reduced exposure with still-large old SOL rights; and new worst windows elsewhere. A jointly adverse BTC/SOL move, beta drift, gaps, IOC partial fill, stale feed or unresolved ACK prevents any hard hedge/DD50 guarantee. There is no logical contradiction in a valid native candidate failing both economic criteria.
