## Minimal causal dataset and proposed primary label

No z/beta/sigma, pending-cache state or exact asset PnL feature is invented. Initial features: asset-presence flags, parent side, elapsed time since first confirmed BTC entry fill, native BTC/SOL quantities, total native cash/equity/UPnL-to-equity, causal running equity drawdown, and count of prior confirmed BTC TP levels. Use only facts timestamped strictly before decision/submission. Quantity is native position quantity; no synthetic per-asset PnL. Parent side/entry time comes from confirmed audit, not latest eventual parent totals. Any field whose causal timestamp cannot be established is missing, not imputed from its final audit value.

Proposed primary binary risk target: within the next 72 hours of S0 native settled receipts, does portfolio W fall at least 10% below the strictly prior event W? Horizon is fixed prospectively for this event-conditioned portfolio task, not copied from old directional labels. Require complete 72h coverage inside the partition and use actual receipt availability, not a later receipt backdated to its source. Do not inspect U to complete V labels. Keep continuous adverse W change as descriptive audit, not an alternative selection target. This is risk under original S0 continuation; it is NOT the value of reduce/skip. No final ROI/DD or action-value claim until a separately admitted full native run.

A data-availability guard: prior receipt must be strictly earlier than event and at most two minutes old, with source < receipt; reject ambiguous same-boundary native fills/unknown freshness rather than merge them. Record count rejected and reason. Future native W alone uses native recorded total, no per-asset allocation. Connected BTC/SOL events sharing a parent OR overlapping 72h portfolio-outcome intervals are one dependence block, including different parents/assets.
