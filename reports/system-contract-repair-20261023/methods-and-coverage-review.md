## What the existing methods establish

The audit reports actual application of source identity review, archival ablation/Pareto screening, two common-base factorial contrasts, risk attribution, and recovery/migration checks. These are more than a bibliography. Their limits remain material: no new native runs or fits, no complete selection universe/PBO, no identified missing factorial cells, no proof that each historical threshold is useful, and no forward validation. The reported hedge-ratio identity is a one-step covariance diagnostic; it is not an executable hedge policy or a drawdown bound. Deleting fills and retaining their downstream equity is not a valid counterfactual.

[NIST's design guidance](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/pri/section3/pri3345.htm) distinguishes screening main effects from estimating interactions. Applied here: H_SOL and H_BTC require a common execution/control base, and a missing combination cannot be inferred from their separate outcomes. The six-run budget does not justify inventing cells. [Boyd et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/1705.00109) separate forecasts from allocation under risk and trading/holding costs. Applied here: execution correctness, risk accounting and predictive usefulness require different evidence; the paper validates no CoinMaster coefficient or DD target. These are the only new external sources consulted in this review.
