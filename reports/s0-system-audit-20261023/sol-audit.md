## What S0 actually does

The exact saved candidate uses EMA34 BTC direction, 270-day beta, 65-day relative return and 180-day z history, SOL entries at signed z **1.25/2.5/3.75**, independent level budgets **1.75/2.625/3.5 H**, half/full mean-reversion exits **0.375/0.125**, and a **14-day** holding timeout. Original SOL is enabled; `research_sol_cover=null`, conditional SOL false, fixed-episode-beta signal mode false. Newer guard/residual/budget experiments are not S0.

**Signal:** beta is Cov(daily log SOL returns, daily log BTC returns)/Var(daily log BTC returns), fitted over 270 completed daily returns. Validity requires **0.2<beta<4**. Relative return is log(SOL/SOL65daysago) minus current beta times log(BTC/BTC65daysago). The mean and population standard deviation use the prior 180 relative observations, excluding the current residual from that normalization window. Once SOL first fills, sigma is frozen for the SOL ownership cycle, while relative return and its mean continue changing. `episode_fixed_beta=false` means the daily z signal continues using the rolling current beta; the **size denominator H uses the entry beta**. These are different roles.

For BTC-long, positive residual extremes permit SOL-short; for BTC-short, negative residual extremes permit SOL-long. That is a mean-reversion proposition conditional on the BTC regime. It is not a direct trigger that BTC is losing money or that portfolio DD needs protection. Beta bounds are not a correlation floor, nor proof that the estimated relationship will hold during stress. The exact archive does not export the full daily correlation/z/mu/frozen-sigma decision path needed to independently measure stress-time relationship stability.

**Metadata trap:** `split_cover_audit.beta_method` says `720_COMPLETED_1H_SIMPLE_RETURNS_SOL_ON_BTC_FROZEN_PER_BTC_EPISODE`, but the same object has `cover=[]` and SOL-cover is disabled. That label belongs to the unused cover implementation. Exact S0 source and candidate establish the **270-day daily log-return** overlay above. Its actual entry betas are explicitly saved in all 105 `split_cover_audit.parents[*].planref_v1_beta` records; those records, rather than the generic label, are used below.

**Rights:** positive BTC TP levels create SOL rights only through confirmed native BTC reduction fills; partial reductions earn proportional rights. Omitted zero-wave levels are a separate actual-entry-backed route: confirmed net BTC entry earns fractional entitlement, then the causal BTC quote must touch the deferred q0 reference in the favorable direction, with paired current-session bars, before activation. An unfilled intention does not create exposure authority. Original eligibility persists after the earning decision (`sol_late_entry_after_tp=true`); it is not restricted to the TP-fill day. Thus rights may survive after substantial BTC reduction or BTC flattening. Submissions and fills are distinct; native min-lot, freshness, queued-order ownership and margin gates still apply.

**Size:** H = gross reference BTC quantity × actual blended BTC entry VWAP / frozen entry beta. PLANREF P0 controls BTC management, not this money numerator. Gross reference is preserved when zero targets reduce accepted net BTC quantity. Per level the still-unfilled allowance is H×multiplier×earned fraction minus filled notional and recorded consumed/retired credits. These are additive level allowances: their sum can reach **7.875H** before other constraints, not a 3.5H aggregate ceiling. The native order path limits each SOL-add request by the candidate 30m parent notional and rounds down to lots; it does not retarget aggregate SOL to remaining BTC/beta. The remaining-BTC hedge gate and SOL risk cap branches are off.

**Exits:** regime reversal and the BTC profit-trail group-close rule can close both assets. The SOL hard timeout is mandatory and preempts queued ordinary work even though a historical candidate flag remains false. Half-exit at signed z≤0.375 is followed by full-exit only on a later decision index at signed z≤0.125, after the half has actually completed. Mean reversion may therefore close a profitable or losing position; these are not price-loss limits. Timeout is anchored to first SOL ownership/fill, not reset by each add. Ordinary planned SOL exits use native taker IOC, with native partial-fill/retry ownership. BTC TP3 flattening does **not** universally force SOL-flat: the SOL-only endpoint below is direct evidence. No SOL price/z stop, portfolio loss limit, deposit reserve or automatic deleveraging cap is enabled.

**Margin and reinvestment:** BTC40x/SOL20x and current public Bybit tiers are native fixture assumptions, not economic hedge ratios. Before increases the injected margin policy evaluates prospective IM+MM, native reservations/free margin, positive marked equity and max gross/equity50; reductions bypass increase gates. These are admission checks, not a 30–50% continuous DD promise. Native marked active equity sizes later BTC episodes; initial active allocation100%, reserve0, transfers0 and deposit protection off. SOL gains/losses therefore change later BTC dollar sizes even when signal dates/directions remain identical. H follows original gross reference size, and existing exposure is not automatically scaled down with falling equity.

## Implication for the 30–50% DD / ≥90% profit objective

A useful design question is how much **residual directional SOL risk** the strategy should retain after BTC reductions, independently of its entry alpha proposition. That question cannot be answered by labeling the whole SOL position a hedge, subtracting its eventual profit, or clipping historical fixed quantities and calling the result a portfolio. The exact 90%-net-profit TOTAL threshold is **5,470,396.514987347**. The current archive supplies no validated policy meeting it with DD≤50%; DD30 remains aspirational.

Existing native experiments already constrain simplistic answers. `reports/btc-split-sol-cover-20261018/README.md` finds that replacing the selective overlay with permanent base hedging loses substantial carry/alpha and changes later BTC quantities through equity. `reports/immediate-sol-insurance-113/analysis-report.md` independently finds joint losses and costly insurance on profitable BTC episodes. `reports/e4-sol-residual-20261023/REPORT.md` and `reports/e4-budget-tp3-20261023/REPORT.md` show that cutting targeted SOL stress can improve October/January while introducing a larger later BTC drawdown on changed capital. These are distinct tested strategies; their failures do not prove that every SOL risk rule must fail, and their localized improvements do not establish S0-wide50/90 acceptance.

Missing witnesses for a stronger conclusion are: signed asset funding; native realized/close-uPnL components per asset at each receipt; complete SOL rights/ownership and z/mu/frozen-sigma decision exports; actual minute beta coverage; realized stress/regime correlation and beta stability; and executable intraminute depth/mark/liquidation fidelity. The source's selective gating and native cash bridges are established; structural alpha independence, continuous insurance effectiveness and a profit-preserving numerical risk cap are not.
