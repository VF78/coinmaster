## Definitions and limitations

- Per-trade gross/net reused from archived actual-native weighted-average fill attribution, not native settled per-asset cash/PnL export.
- Signed funding UNKNOWN; native TOTAL minus seed minus confirmed fill net is unallocated, not automatically funding.
- Trade means continuous per-asset native flat-to-flat inventory: additions and partial TP exits are not additional trades. All 105 BTC parent books exactly match native flat-to-flat cycles.
- Return denominator is cumulative entry notional, including additions/re-additions. This is not return on equity or capital/margin allocation. Asset returns are not additive.
- Size-weighted return = SUM(trade net ex funding)/SUM(cumulative trade entry notional)*100.
- Distribution statistics use float descriptive arithmetic; monetary conservation uses Decimal. Quantiles use linear interpolation at (n-1)*p; standard deviation is population.
- Monthly closed-trade tables assign entire trade and its entry/exit fees to final exit month; do not equal calendar account equity PnL.
- Native CSV fill side/liquidity/order IDs are matched one-to-one with audited time/instrument/qty/rounded-price; exact audit prices/notional/commissions used for money.
- No native backtest/optimizer/trading code/config/service changes. No raw full-minute gzip read by this report.
- Partial exit fill means a native fill after which inventory remains; several fills at one timestamp may implement one strategy close decision, so these are not counts of distinct exit decisions.
- Side-specific streaks are within the filtered side subset; intervening trades on the opposite side are omitted, not counted as breaks.
- SOL timeout/rights/anchor provenance is known only where archived audit explicitly records it; fill reasons alone do not prove full rights ledger or why no order occurred.
