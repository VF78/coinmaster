## Common decision and action contract

Use the 1,529 existing actual-held R3 H4 decision states as a **reused discovery calendar**, not fresh OOS and not the complete early-capital population. At completed H4 close t, s is actual pre-t BTC side (+1 long, −1 short). Bars are completed execution OHLC, available at t. Earliest conservative action execution is t+60s, from execution row open=t; reconstruct/recheck actual pre-execution quantity and reject ambiguous same-timestamp order. Input feature availability must precede execution. Do not condition entry on future flat, trade result or future drawdown.

One common 24h action horizon, capped by actual BTC flat; no horizon search. Each family independently gets its first qualifying opportunity per actual BTC episode, with no simultaneous/future trigger selection and no re-add. Same common action library: no action; opposite-side SOL HALF_MV with NS=.5*NB*cov720/varSOL720; and equal-factor BTC trim with relief=NS*beta720=.5*rho720²*NB. This is the previously defined minimum-variance convention, not half factor or half owner volatility. Require finite positive lag variances, beta>0 and rho>0 for this library; insufficient history means ineligible, never zero-filled. Root may choose a different single library **before** outcomes.

Account actual BTC reductions, side changes, flat and retained quantity; proportionally reduce SOL with BTC, close at flat, never retain excess SOL. Trim avoids the native BTC cash/fees it actually removes; hypothetical restoration at 24h is capped by BTC still held. Include both assets' market/IOC taker fees, signed realized funding, explicit spread/slippage and entry/exit costs. Boundary funding alternatives and event-clock UNKNOWN must survive. Native three-TP events are observed reductions, not synthetic replacement exits. No action library demonstrates margin/admission, compounded TOTAL, native event DD or deployability.

## Exact notation and availability

For X in BTC/SOL, C_X(t), H_X(t), L_X(t), V_X(t), T_X(t) are completed H4 execution close/high/low/volume/turnover. H1 closes support r_X1 and beta720. r_X4=C_X(t)/C_X(t−4h)−1; A iff s*r_B4<0. Past windows below exclude the current H4 return/bar unless stated. beta720, rho720, sigma720 use exactly 720 H1 return ends t−720h,...,t−1h, SIMPLE returns. Funding uses last publication strictly before t, never the next rate. Mark and execution basis uses synchronized prior-minute closes available at t, with no retained older mark silently promoted to t.

## Selection and knowledge gaps before root freezes labels

1. Root must freeze the admitted rows, transformations, one size/horizon, episode selection, cost cases and early cohort definition before loading outcomes. No adaptive threshold/window changes, and no combinations chosen from profitable subsets in this pass. Trigger coverage/missingness may be checked outcome-blind; empty/sparse gates are reported as such, not repaired with a new quantile.
2. Primary estimand is paired SOL minus equal-factor trim net bp per actual BTC episode; also show both versus no action and total cash/turnover. Separate risk-reduction evidence from capital-growth evidence. A gate that only identifies losses is not evidence SOL improves on trim. Include every eligible episode and early flat, not just full horizons or profitable hedges.
3. Count **all tried formulas, both R0 signs if read, cost/scenario selection and failed/empty tests** in the ledger. Family clustering does not erase the extra selection cost. One conservative primary multiplicity correction may use Holm over the complete admitted formula list, with episode-clustered uncertainty; use neither 1,529 rows as independent trades nor correlation of tests as permission to shrink correction. For sparse episodes report descriptive uncertainty/INCONCLUSIVE rather than manufacture significance. Root chooses the inferential implementation before labels.
4. First/second-year and early-cohort tables are prespecified stability diagnostics; both years are already viewed history. They cannot become fresh heldout evidence. Repeated full-period discovery needs genuinely unseen future data for confirmation; resampling does not create OOS. A family selected on all history remains discovery even if a confidence interval passes.
5. Existing prices/actual held states do not establish executable SOL capacity, bid/ask spread, intraminute peaks, synchronized terminal marks, own-capital admission or joint event DD. Preserve UNKNOWN_DATA_CLOCK_TERMINAL_ENDPOINT unless new causal raw evidence resolves it. Current sample omits early September due to lag720 and begins only in R3 cycle5; it cannot establish restoration of the original early capital/compounding mechanism.
6. Local passing action arithmetic can only nominate a candidate for a separate native contract/budget decision. Original absolute 70%/90% S0 floors, <=50% settled and coherent-event DD, full accounting and zero model liquidation remain unchanged. No native/full/short/training budget is opened by this design.
