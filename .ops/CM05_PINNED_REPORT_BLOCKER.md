It rejects transport/malformed/unknown status, lost-ACK CLOID mismatch, foreign or unknown open orders/fills/positions, duplicate identities, missing retained anchor, non-USDC fill fee, capped same-millisecond windows and excess pagination. A bounded response below the published cap is only conditional evidence: Info has no cross-endpoint atomic snapshot/cursor, no proof against silent omissions below cap, and its 10000-fill retention can outrun a durable checkpoint.
